Health Indicators Tracker

Instant validation of your vital biometrics against internationally recognized healthy ranges.

Body Mass Index

Measure of body fat based on height and weight that applies to adult men and women.

Normal reference range: 18.5 – 24.9

Blood Pressure

The pressure of circulating blood against the walls of blood vessels.

Normal reference target: Below 120/80 mmHg

Total Cholesterol

Measure of total amount of cholesterol in your blood, including LDL and HDL.

Normal reference target: Less than 200 mg/dL

Blood Sugar Level

Measure of glucose in the blood, an important screening for diabetes risk.

Normal reference target: 70 – 130 mg/dL (before meals/fasting)

Waist-to-Hip Ratio

Calculated by dividing waist circumference by hip circumference to assess weight distribution.

Normal reference target: < 0.85 (female), < 0.90 (male)

Resting Heart Rate

Number of times your heart beats per minute while at complete rest.

Normal reference range: 60 – 100 bpm
